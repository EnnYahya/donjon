## Floor styles

**Floors 1-5: The Fallen Halls**. Narrow corridors, ruined halls, simple traps.
1. Broken Entrance, 2. Collapsed Corridor, 3. Dark Library, 4. The Old Chapel, 5. The Forgotten Mines

**Floors 6-10: The Ruined Catacombs**. Complex paths, faster monsters, hidden rooms.
6. The Sewer Depths, 7. The Watchtower, 8. The Poison Forest, 9. The Abandoned Village, 10. The Execution Pit

**Floors 11-15: The Ice Depths**. Freezing patches slow you down.
11. The Frozen Tombs, 12. The Crystal Caves, 13. The Sand Ruins, 14. The Clockworks, 15. The Storm Keep

**Floors 16-20: The Lava Caverns**. Lava pools burn. Stronger enemies.
16. The Magma Forge, 17. The Forgotten Arena, 18. The Rotting Gardens, 19. The Astral Hall, 20. The Black Monastery

**Floors 21-25: The Sinking Shrine**. Flooded floors, ambushes, key and door puzzles.
21. The Drowned Sanctum, 22. The Graveyard, 23. The Alchemy Labs, 24. The Dimensions Rift, 25. The Spider Nest

**Floors 26-30: The Dark Zones**. Almost no light. Elite monsters, more traps.
26. The Demon Forge, 27. The Lost Kingdom, 28. The Giant's Tomb, 29. The Void Gallery, 30. The Cursed Library

**Floors 31-35: The Forgotten Temple**. A complex maze full of ancient traps.
31. The Eternal Storm, 32. The Hellish Mines, 33. The Demon Cathedral, 34. The Crystal Tower, 35. The Endless Bridge

**Floors 36-40: The Blood Cathedral**. Almost no safe areas. Aggressive monsters.
36. The Bone Crypt, 37. The Sunken Temple, 38. The Dragon's Lair, 39. The Void Gate, 40. The Ossuary Gate

**Floors 41-45: The Void Realm**. Shadows, illusions, nothing to hold on to.
41. The Weeping Abyss, 42. The Ashen Nave, 43. The Mirror Maze, 44. The Rotting Road, 45. The Wailing Depths

**Floors 46-50: The Madness Realm**. The final area. The Madness Ring waits.
46. The Hollow Court, 47. The Crimson Stair, 48. The Mad Gallery, 49. The Last Corridor, 50. The Madness Throne
