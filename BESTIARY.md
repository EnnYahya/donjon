# Bestiary of the Abyss

28 horrors live in the dungeon. The first 8 were there from the start, the next 20 appear as you go deeper.
Every monster has its own way of moving and killing you: read the wind-up, then roll (Space), block (right click) or back off.

## The first 8
| Monster | Floors | How it acts |
|---|---|---|
| The Hollow | 1+ | Slow, hunched wanderer. Swings or lunges. |
| The Stalker | 4+ | Fast, long-headed, tracks your scent. 3-hit flurry or lunge. |
| The Blind One | 6+ | Cannot see, hears everything. Ground smash or wide sweep. |
| The Mimic | 2+ | A chest identical to a real one. Opens like a chest, then bites. |
| The Warden | 9+ | Armored guard. Smash, wide sweep, overhead swing. |
| The Shadow | 12+ | Appears in the dark, vanishes if watched, lunges when you look away. |
| Floor Guardian | 5, 10, 15 ... | Boss: slam, charge, swing. |
| The Elden Guardian | 50 | Final boss: slam, charge, orb volleys, two phases. |
