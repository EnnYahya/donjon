of the Elden Ring

Fifty floors. One ring. No way out.

Before you descend

This dungeon does not forgive.

  • If you die, the run is over. You lose your pack, your gear and all floor progress.
  • You restart on Floor 1 in a brand new, different dungeon.
  • The stairs are guarded. Listen. Light is fuel. Healing is rare.
  • Reaching the Elden Ring returns you to Floor 1, and the dungeon grows harsher with every loop.

Survive

  • Find the purple glowing stairs on each floor and descend. 50 floors, then the Elden Ring.
  • Monsters hunt by sight and sound. Walking is quiet, sprinting and fighting are loud.
  • Hit an unaware enemy for a backstab (daggers are best).
  • Your lantern burns down. Keep oil, or find torches.
  • Some chests bite. Look closely.
  • Pressure plates click before they fire. Step away.
  • Locked gates need keys found elsewhere on the floor.
  • Scratches and sounds behind a wall may hide a secret room.
  • Seeds: add ?seed=1234 to the address to replay a dungeon.
